## Natural Earth

`data/land.geojson` contains Natural Earth land geometry, copied from
`medieval-america-map/sources/natural-earth-land.geojson` in the source
repository at commit `14045e3f44ba50e7409750e3c66c566c50be542c`, with unused
feature properties removed. This is the global source geography, not the
fictional territory layers or the cropped American Kingdoms atlas.
