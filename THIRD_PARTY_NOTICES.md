## Natural Earth and US Census geography

Natural Earth geography is public domain: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/.

The blank land layer and physical North America layers originate in the American Kingdoms repository's Natural Earth source/data files. Modern Massachusetts county boundaries and some reference boundaries are based on US Census Bureau data. Source county service: https://tigerweb.geo.census.gov/arcgis/rest/services/Generalized_ACS2024/State_County/MapServer/11.

## Supplied Europe reference

`world-map/assets/europe-reference.webp` and `europe-reference-lite.webp` are optimized copies of the user-supplied “Worldbuilding Map Base - Europe Kaiserreich & 1914.jpg”. Europe territory outlines are approximate derivatives traced from that reference. The reference and derived example map content retain their source rights; no blanket MIT grant is asserted for them.

The Europe 1936 map is illustrative, not a canonical reconstruction. No affiliation with or endorsement by the Kaiserreich project is implied. Generic example flags were newly created for the template, not copied from Kaiserreich.
